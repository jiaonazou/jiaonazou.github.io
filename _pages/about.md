I am a Unit Process Development Engineer at Infineon Technologies Austria, focused on plasma etching for SiC device technologies including MOSFETs, JFETs, and super junctions.

My work combines cleanroom experimentation, statistically driven DoE studies, and process integration to improve etch uniformity, CD control, yield, and manufacturing robustness. I support tool qualification and ramp-up activities using FMEA, root-cause analysis, and continuous improvement methods.

I am particularly interested in connecting semiconductor process development with practical data workflows. In my current role, I developed a KNIME-based inline CD analysis application to accelerate process evaluation and improve data visibility, and I use simulation-supported recipe optimization to reduce risk during process changes.

Before joining industry, I completed a PhD in Semiconductor Material at the University of Freiburg, where I researched crystal growth and characterization of II-VI semiconductors for detector and optical applications.

## Current focus

- Plasma etching process optimization for SiC technologies
- Process window definition through design of experiments
- Defect and metrology-driven process control
- Data-enabled decision support for manufacturing

## Methods and tools

- Characterization: SEM, XRD, AFM, EDX, XPS, optical microscopy
- Electrical testing: I-V, Hall, Van der Pauw, DLTS (cryogenic)
- Data and software: MATLAB, KNIME, CEDA, AutoCAD
